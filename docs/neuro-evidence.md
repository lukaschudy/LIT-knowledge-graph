# Neuro research demonstration: Vici/EPG5 and adjacent autophagy assays

This is a small, source-grounded assay-reuse discussion set, not a clinical recommendation. It anchors on EPG5-related Vici syndrome and autophagosome–lysosome fusion. The candidate assay is the tandem fluorescent LC3 reporter the authors used in EPG5-knockout HeLa cells: they quantified RFP/GFP fluorescence colocalization and describe the reporter as indicating reduced fusion. This is a cellular proxy interpreted by the authors as fusion evidence; it does not directly image membrane merger. In the same study, patient skin fibroblasts showed partial autophagic dysfunction, but the cited tandem-reporter method was performed in EPG5-knockout HeLa cells. The request therefore specifies that HeLa model context; it does not imply patient-derived Vici material is available. [Primary Vici/EPG5 study, abstract and Figure 5 legend](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5471274/).

Two comparator neighborhoods are included, with their distinct steps retained:

- **BPAN / WDR45**: the paper places WDR45 in early autophagosome formation and reports high-content LC3-puncta imaging in a patient-derived iPSC midbrain dopaminergic model. LC3 puncta are described as representative of autophagosomes; this readout does not test the EPG5 fusion step. [Primary BPAN model and assay paper, Introduction, Abstract, and assay method](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10515824/).
- **SPG47 / AP4B1**: the AP-4 literature describes AP4B1-associated SPG47 and AP-4-dependent ATG9A trafficking. The comparator assay quantifies ATG9A distribution relative to the TGN, using high-content imaging in patient fibroblasts. This is a cargo-trafficking readout, not a fusion assay. [Primary AP-4 screening paper, Introduction and imaging method](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10794252/); [primary diagnostic fibroblast assay paper, Abstract and Figure 3 legend](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8557665/).

The proposed EPG5 reporter reuse is limited to a feasibility discussion in the specified HeLa model.
