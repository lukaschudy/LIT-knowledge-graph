# GRIN2B overview and support signposts

“Ask about this” inserts an editable, entity-specific question about what the knowledge graph records and where to seek help. In the hosted GRIN demo, gene-level GRIN2B knowledge/help questions receive a deterministic curated response. Variant-specific comparisons keep their detailed source-annotation answer. Clinical requests retain their existing boundary.

The summary derives membership counts and classification citations from the deployed annotation bundle. It distinguishes individual variant categories, including provisional and opposing controls, from claims about the whole gene. The support links below are maintained separately from graph evidence; they do not create accepted relationships or imply clinical eligibility, current recruitment or guaranteed access.

Official pages checked on 2026-10-04:

- [GRIN2B Foundation: Connect With Us](https://grin2b.com/connect-with-us/) — parent and caregiver community.
- [Simons Searchlight: GRIN2B](https://www.simonssearchlight.org/research/what-we-study/grin2b/) — gene guide and voluntary research participation; includes guidance to share resources with clinicians.
- [GRIN2B Foundation: For Researchers](https://grin2b.com/for-researchers/) — research resources and clinical-center listings. Users contact the organizations about access.

These links are a curated snapshot, not a live availability check. The local model workspace keeps its separate source-grounded answer pipeline; the deterministic overview is implemented in the hosted annotation API.
