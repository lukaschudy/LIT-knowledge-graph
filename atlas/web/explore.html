Drag to rotate. Shift-drag to pan. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Select a node to follow its connections; select a line to inspect its evidence.

Search makes every node keyboard-accessible. On the canvas, arrow keys rotate, + / − zoom, and 0 fits the graph. Colors distinguish record types. Their positions and distances are a layout choice, not an embedding or a measure of biological similarity.

Lines are recorded assertions. Dashed lines are inferred; rust lines have counter-evidence or negative assertions. opens source search, claim review, and plan gates.

Harvested graph pages have a selectable per-page size. Browse further pages, expand one selected neighborhood, or search the full index. Browse source records to inspect rows that are not mapped to graph nodes. Harvested records are raw source data, not reviewed scientific assertions.

Ask Atlas uses the configured model and cited-source corpus. A harvested node selection is text context only, and retrieval may not cover all harvest records. Search results are leads, not approved graph claims. It is not a general-purpose AI or clinical advice service.
